## Short description (≤ 160 chars)
Start with $100 on a 3D city street. Take risky deals, dodge decline, hire partners and climb 11 wealth classes from survival to untouchable billionaire.

## Description (long)
You have $100, a pair of worn sneakers and one street ahead of you. How far can you climb?

**Broke to Billionaire: The $100 Start** is a quick-decision money-climbing game set on living 3D streets in six world cities: Taipei, Tokyo, Las Vegas, Singapore, New York and Monaco. Every step brings a new card: a project to invest in, a street job, a shady casino, a hospital, a shop, a chance meeting with a partner, or a local story. Pick your stake, read the odds, and hit INVEST.

Climb through 11 wealth classes, from Survival and Worker to Magnate, Oligarch and Untouchable. Every class unlocks new mechanics, a new UI frame, new music and bigger deals. Fall below the line and the perks are taken back one by one.

But time is the real currency. Every rest ages you and raises your **decline risk**. Lose all your hearts and the run ends. Spend on vitamins, check-ups, retreats and experimental biotech, but every health purchase costs more than the last and nothing is guaranteed.

**Features**
- 6 hand-styled 3D cities with their own buildings, music, deals and rules
- 11 wealth classes with unlock ceremonies and class-specific interfaces
- 15+ partners with their own stories and specialties: street-food legends, garage founders, hedge-fund traders, gamblers, art dealers, herbalists, fitness coaches and longevity scientists
- Health and decline system: hospitals sell paid chances, not guarantees
- Shops, black markets, casinos, auctions, luxuries and a trophy cabinet for LV victory points
- Regional stories that never repeat within a run, and random modifiers that make every run different
- Rest phases with mini-games, bills and upkeep. Keep enough cash to survive them
- Works offline, on desktop and mobile (landscape)

Can you turn $100 into a dynasty before time runs out?

## Controls
- **Mouse / Touch:** tap cards and buttons to choose, drag the stake slider, tap INVEST
- **Space / Enter:** confirm or continue
- **Esc:** menu / pause
- Walk forward with the big ➜ / NEXT button; tap people and places on the street to interact
